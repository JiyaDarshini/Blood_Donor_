How Blood SOS Works

A seamless, high-speed emergency response protocol bridging donors, patients, and healthcare facilities.

🚨

1. Instant Emergency SOS

Patients or hospitals submit an SOS request specifying required blood group, units, hospital address, and urgency level.

🧬

2. ABO/Rh Smart Matching

Our medical-grade compatibility engine filters available donors within the geographic radius based on red blood cell transfusion rules.

🔔

3. Direct Donor Alert

Matched donors receive in-app alerts and notifications to accept or decline the request, protecting personal contact privacy until mutual consent.

🎖️

4. Verified Rewards

Following hospital confirmation, administrators verify the donation, awarding LifePoints and milestone badges to honored donors.
